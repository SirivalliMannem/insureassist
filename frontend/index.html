Risk Assessment Matrix & Guidelines

Underwriting exposure tiers, property scoring models, and loss threshold definitions.

Risk Model v3.2 Active

Low Risk Tier

Preferred rate classification
Score: 0–25
  • 0 claims in preceding 60 months
  • Credit tier 1 (FICO score > 720)
  • Dwelling age < 15 years with modern wiring
  • Standard deductibles ($500–$1,000)
✓ Auto-Eligible for Standard Binding

Medium Risk Tier

Standard rate with conditions
Score: 26–65
  • 1 minor non-weather claim in 36 mos
  • Property age 15–35 years with inspection
  • Increased deductible mandatory ($1,500+)
  • Standard flood/earthquake exclusions apply
⚠ Requires Endorsement Riders

High Risk Tier

Substandard or surplus lines
Score: 66–100
  • 2+ open or recent claims in 24 mos
  • Severe coastal storm or flood zone exposure
  • Commercial liability limits > $2,000,000
  • Mandatory supervisory sign-off before binding
✕ Senior Underwriter Approval Mandatory

Risk Assessment Workflow & Decision Framework

Standardized 5-step underwriting process from submission to binding
1
Submission Ingestion
Receive application, ACORD forms, loss runs, and applicant MVR record.
2
Risk Scoring
Compute automated risk score based on property, claims, and territory metrics.
3
Risk Classification
Assign Low (0–25), Medium (26–65), or High (66–100) exposure rating tier.
4
Guideline Check
Verify inspection reports, deductible rules, and required exclusion riders.
5
Final Underwriting Decision
Execute binding approval, conditional endorsement, or supervisor referral.

Core Decision Evaluation Considerations

Property & Building Condition

Roof condition, plumbing, electrical system, and proximity to fire hydrants.

Claims & Driving History

5-year claims history, previous water/fire losses, driving/telematics history, and MVR record.

Location & Natural Hazards

Flood risk, wildfire risk, coastal wind exposure, and earthquake risk.

Financial & Coverage Limits

Replacement cost, liability coverage, and the underwriter's approval limit.

Underwriting Policy Review & Guidelines

Standard policy forms, coverage endorsement rules, and binding authority matrix.

Policy Guidelines Active

Standard Coverage Inclusions

Mandatory baseline policy coverages
Base Inclusions
  • Dwelling Coverage (Coverage A): Replaces residential structure up to 100% replacement value.
  • Personal Property (Coverage C): Covers interior contents against fire, theft, and windstorm.
  • Personal Liability (Coverage E): $300,000 to $1,000,000 baseline legal liability protection.
  • Loss of Use (Coverage D): Additional living expenses during necessary structural repairs.
Included by default across all standard admitted property & casualty forms.

Standard Exclusions & Endorsements

Items requiring explicit separate riders
Mandatory Riders
  • Flood & Rising Water: Excluded from standard HO-3 forms (Requires NFIP or Private Flood Rider).
  • Earth Movement & Earthquake: Excluded without dedicated seismic rider endorsement.
  • Wear, Tear & Deterioration: Inherent vice and lack of maintenance strictly non-payable.
  • Unlisted Commercial Operations: Business pursuits excluded without commercial endorsement.
Must be explicitly endorsed on declarations page prior to policy issuance.
